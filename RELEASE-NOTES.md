# Yang-Mills Theory and the Mass Gap (v4.44.0)

The paper (227 pages) proves the strong-coupling mass gap m >= arccosh(1 + (kappa_G/(N beta) - 29/2)/12) for every compact simple gauge group and shows that one uniform cube estimate at physical scale l gives every nontrivial continuum limit the mass gap m >= 16 varpi/(625 e^2 l). This version adds gauge-covariant strong mixing at the physical scale and the averaged cube condition, coarse cells in four dimensions, flowed observables at positive flow time, figures, footnotes and complete derivations. Every section, appendix and reference was checked again: the corrections include the constants of the tempered continuum extension, the convergence range in the Dobrushin corollary, the free-field ratio estimates, the infrared-bound constants and the plaquette-correlation reweighting.

The uniform weak-coupling cube estimate (D) and a nontrivial continuum limit (E) remain unproved, so the continuum mass gap is not proved.
