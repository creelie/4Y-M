# Yang-Mills Theory and the Mass Gap (v4.47.1)

Authors: Deep Bhattacharjee, Ushashi Bhattacharya, James Montgomery Lockwood, Tristan Romanov, Ikailo John Sessford and Sanjeevan Singha Roy.

Detailed version, 69 pages, without diagrams. We prove a lattice mass gap with explicit constants for every compact simple gauge group at strong coupling, and that a spectral gap varpi for resampling cubes of physical size l, uniform in the lattice spacing, gives every nontrivial continuum limit the mass gap 16 varpi/(625 e^2 l). The cube gap is proved at strong coupling, for SU(2) up to beta_W = 7/20 by resampling stars and for SU(3) up to 0.45, and is reduced (Theorem D) to a decay of correlations below the sharp threshold 1/2 across one slab of width l/2, which is proved in two dimensions. An appendix shows what curvature, entropy, fixed-block and transfer-matrix methods cannot reach.

The weak-coupling cube estimate (D) and a nontrivial continuum limit (E) remain unproved, so the continuum mass gap is not proved.
