# Yang-Mills Theory and the Mass Gap (v4.45.0)

A short account (22 pages, amsart). Four-dimensional lattice Yang-Mills theory with a compact simple gauge group G in U(N) has the lattice mass gap m >= arccosh(1 + (kappa_G/(N beta) - 29/2)/12) whenever N beta < 2 kappa_G/29. A spectral gap varpi of the dynamics that resamples cubes of physical size l, uniform in the lattice spacing, gives every nontrivial continuum limit the mass gap Delta >= 16 varpi/(625 e^2 l). The cube gap is proved at strong coupling, for SU(2) up to beta_W = 5/18 and for SU(3) up to beta_W = 0.45 by gauge fixing inside each cube, and for massive free fields. The Lean 4 formalisation of I. J. Sessford (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24073388) is credited in the introduction.

The uniform weak-coupling cube estimate (D) and a nontrivial continuum limit (E) remain unproved, so the continuum mass gap is not proved. The longer account is release v4.44.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24085959.
