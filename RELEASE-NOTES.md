# Yang-Mills Theory and the Mass Gap (v4.46.0)

Authors: Deep Bhattacharjee, Ushashi Bhattacharya, James Montgomery Lockwood, Tristan Romanov, Ikailo John Sessford and Sanjeevan Singha Roy. I. J. Sessford contributed the Lean 4 / Mathlib formalisation of the analytic layer (entroptics-mass-gap, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24073388).

We prove that four-dimensional lattice Yang-Mills theory with any compact simple gauge group G in U(N) has the mass gap m >= arccosh(1 + (kappa_G/(N beta) - 29/2)/12) whenever N beta < 2 kappa_G/29, and that a spectral gap varpi for resampling cubes of physical size l, uniform in the lattice spacing, forces the mass gap Delta >= 16 varpi/(625 e^2 l) in every nontrivial continuum limit. We prove this cube gap at strong coupling, for SU(2) up to beta_W = 5/18 and for SU(3) up to beta_W = 0.45, and for massive free fields.

The uniform weak-coupling cube estimate (D) and a nontrivial continuum limit (E) remain unproved, so the continuum mass gap is not proved. The longer account is release v4.44.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24085959.
