# Yang-Mills Theory and the Mass Gap

Deep Bhattacharjee, Ushashi Bhattacharya, James Montgomery Lockwood, Tristan Romanov, Ikailo John Sessford and Sanjeevan Singha Roy.

DOI (all versions): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23444338

- [PDF](papers/YangMills.pdf): 22 pages, amsart.
- [TeX source](papers/YangMillstex.zip): compile main.tex with pdfLaTeX, two passes.

Four-dimensional lattice Yang-Mills theory with a compact simple gauge group G in U(N) has the lattice mass gap m >= arccosh(1 + (kappa_G/(N beta) - 29/2)/12) whenever N beta < 2 kappa_G/29. A spectral gap varpi of the dynamics that resamples cubes of physical size l, uniform in the lattice spacing, gives every nontrivial continuum limit the mass gap Delta >= 16 varpi/(625 e^2 l); the cube gap holds at strong coupling (SU(2) up to beta_W = 5/18, SU(3) up to 0.45) and for massive free fields.

The cube estimate (D) along the weak-coupling tuning and a nontrivial continuum limit (E) are not proved, so the mass gap of the continuum theory is not proved here. The longer account (228 pages) is release v4.44.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24085959.
