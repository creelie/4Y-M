# Yang-Mills Theory and the Mass Gap

Deep Bhattacharjee, Ushashi Bhattacharya, James Montgomery Lockwood, Tristan Romanov, Ikailo John Sessford and Sanjeevan Singha Roy.

DOI (all versions): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23444338

- [PDF](papers/YangMills.pdf): 69 pages, amsart.
- [TeX source](papers/YangMillstex.zip): compile main.tex with pdfLaTeX, three passes.

Lattice mass gap with explicit constants for every compact simple gauge group at strong coupling; a cube spectral gap of physical size l, uniform in the lattice spacing, gives every nontrivial continuum limit the mass gap 16 varpi/(625 e^2 l); the cube gap is proved at strong coupling (SU(2) up to beta_W = 7/20) and reduced to a decay of correlations below 1/2 across one slab of width l/2, proved in two dimensions. The continuum mass gap, (D) and (E), are not proved.
