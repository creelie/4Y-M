# Yang-Mills Theory and the Mass Gap

Deep Bhattacharjee, Ushashi Bhattacharya, James Montgomery Lockwood and Tristan Romanov.

DOI (all versions): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23444338

- [PDF](papers/ym4d.pdf): 188 pages, REVTeX 4.2, two columns.
- [TeX source](papers/ym4dtex.zip): compile main.tex with pdfLaTeX, three passes.

We prove that four-dimensional lattice Yang-Mills theory with any compact simple gauge group G in U(N) has the mass gap m >= arccosh(1 + (kappa_G/(N beta) - 29/2)/12) > 0 at strong coupling beta, and that one uniform estimate with constant varpi on cubes of physical side l gives every continuum limit a mass gap of at least 16 varpi/(625 e^2 l).

The cube estimate (D) along the weak-coupling tuning and a nontrivial continuum limit (E) are not proved, so the mass gap of the continuum theory is not proved here. The multiscale material of earlier versions is in the Hexagon preprint 2610.00341v7.
