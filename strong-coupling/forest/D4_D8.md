# Item 5: scoping D4 and D8 (November 2026)

This is item 5 of `ROADMAP.md` §7. Neither piece is attacked. The note records:
- what each piece reduces to after items 1–4;
- one quantitative observation (§2), which shows that the coarsest step of any proof of (D) sits at a coupling the
  weak-coupling estimates here cannot reach.

Status labels:
- §1 is a reduction (sketch);
- §2 is a heuristic count (one-loop running, no theorem);
- §3 is a list.

## 1. D4 after items 1–4

D4 is the mass off the chart on fibres with no calm completion (Remark `chartdecayopen`(a)). After `ISLANDS.md` it
is posed on windows: under an arbitrary exterior, the window W₁ either has a calm completion or does not.
- **Calm completion.** Proposition L (sketch) bounds the off-chart mass by ε_loc, with constants independent of the
  volume.
- **No calm completion.** The exterior forces a large plaquette in W₁⁺, so W₁ meets the large-field set.

**So D4 in window form is the large-field part of (I1)** (`ANNEALED.md` §5): the law conditioned on a configuration
whose large-field set meets the window. Two facts are available there, and neither gives a contraction:
- **Corollary `everyfibre`.** Every fibre concentrates near the least action q(F) of its aligned fields, with error
  (5.2·10¹⁰ n_P³/t³)^{45|W|} e^{−3λt/4}. This locates the mass, but a chart around a non-calm minimiser would need
  Lemma `smallframes` at a background with large plaquettes, and that lemma fails there.
- **Single-link coefficients.** They are bounded uniformly in the field (row ≤ 4.5β_W). This is the island cost of
  `ISLANDS.md` §2: a bound, not a decay.

**Averaging over v.** Lemma `mixture` needs the constrained coefficient only on average over the averages v, under
the law of the averages given the exterior: ∫W₁(κ(·|v), κ′(·|v)) ρ(dv) (observed in the OpenGluon note, `CALM_LINK.md`
§1). So D4 is needed only for the ρ-mass of the non-calm fibres, not as a supremum over them. That mass is small
(Corollary `calmexterior`), but the non-calm fibres still contribute W₁ ≤ diam. That contribution is not proportional to
the boundary perturbation δ, so averaging does not remove D4. It does make D4 an averaged statement, and §2 of
`CALM_LINK.md` uses the averaging to turn the shell term of the second kind of link into a hazard-rate bound.

In Bałaban's approach a large-field region is not given a chart. It is a summation variable, with a weight e^{−cβK²}
per site and analytic coupling to its small-field complement. D4 and the large-field half of D7 are therefore one
problem, and it is the same problem as (I1).

## 2. D8: the coarsest step is at strong-to-intermediate coupling (heuristic)

D8 asks for three things (`OPEN.md` §4):
- the cube condition for the averages ⇒ the law one scale up;
- the cube condition for the coarsest averages;
- the crossover.

Corollary `compose` bounds a cube coefficient by the constrained part plus the response C^ρ of the law of the
averages. Bounding C^ρ is again a cube condition, for the averages one scale up. The recursion has to stop at a base
case: a cube of c blocks per side whose coefficients are bounded directly. Every bound in hand needs c ≥ 17C/α. This
gives:
- c ≥ 5.7·10¹⁴ from C_G (`D5.md`);
- c ≥ 2.2·10¹⁵ from the Gaussian constrained cube;
- c ≥ 10⁸²⁷/α from the Gaussian bound at every scale.

**The count.**
- By the paper's continuum step, (D) at physical size ℓ gives a gap ≥ log(1/α)/(2ℓ) for every continuum limit.
- If the limit has gap m, then ℓ ≥ log(1/α)/(2m).
- The base-case blocks have size ℓ/c.
- With one-loop SU(2) running, β_W(μ) = 0.3715 log(μ/Λ), their effective coupling is at most

  β_top ≤ 0.3715 log(2mc/(Λ log(1/α))).

`d5/scales.out` (α = 0.9) gives:

| base case c from | m/Λ = 1 | m/Λ = 10 | m/Λ = 100 |
|---|---|---|---|
| C_G (5.7·10¹⁴) | 13.7 | 14.6 | 15.4 |
| Gaussian constrained (2.2·10¹⁵) | 14.2 | 15.1 | 15.9 |
| Gaussian all scales (10⁸²⁷/α) | 709 | 709 | 710 |

Every weak-coupling estimate in hand needs a far larger β:

| estimate | threshold |
|---|---|
| `fibrechart`/`chartdecay` | β ≥ 3.8·10³⁸ |
| off-chart window (`ISLANDS.md`) | β ≥ 3.8·10⁹⁷ |
| islands | β ≥ 8.3·10¹⁶³ |

Reaching β ≥ 3.8·10³⁸ at the base case would need log c ≥ 10³⁹. That base case is a cube of e^{10³⁹} blocks, which is
not a base case at all: bounding it is the decay problem again.

**Conclusion (heuristic).**
- The recursion of D8 must end at an effective coupling of order 10 if the base case uses the cube sides we can
  prove, and of order 700 even with the Gaussian all-scale constant.
- That is 36 or more orders of magnitude below every weak-coupling threshold here.
- On the other side, it is far above the strong-coupling range proved by Theorem `blocks` (β_W ≤ 0.351).

So the base case of D8 is the crossover of `OPEN.md` §2, and no sharpening of the weak-coupling constants removes it.
β_top depends on c only logarithmically, so improving C_G or the Gaussian constant moves β_top by tens at most.

**What this does not say.** The effective coupling of block variables is not a Wilson β. One-loop running is not a
theorem. m and Λ are not known to exist. The count only shows where the proof must stand at its last step. It does
not show that the step is false.

## 3. What a D8 attack would need

Listed so the size of the problem is on record. None of it is started.

1. **(H_k) Effective-action bounds at every scale.** The law of the scale-k averages has the form
   e^{−S_k/g_k² + R_k}, where R_k is local and small in a norm that controls Dobrushin coefficients. This is
   Bałaban's small-field output, and it is not available in a form usable here. Given (H_k), a perturbation of
   Theorem `constrainedscales` should give D_c at scale k. That step looks like a Helffer–Sjöstrand computation of the
   `chartdecay` type (plausible, unchecked).
2. **The large-field expansion at every scale.** This is (I1) at scale k (§1).
3. **The base case at β_top ≈ 10–700 (§2).** It needs one of:
   - a strong-coupling-type criterion that holds at that coupling (Track A reaches 0.351 for SU(2), so this is out of
     range);
   - a certified finite-box computation in the crossover (`OPEN.md` §2).

## 4. Effect on the roadmap

- D4 merges with (I1) and with the large-field half of D7. It is one wall, not three.
- D8 contains the crossover as its base case. That case lands at an effective coupling of order 10–700, whatever
  weak-coupling constants are proved.
- So, beyond the items already done, (D) needs:
  - Bałaban-type small- and large-field expansions, for (H_k) and (I1);
  - a nonperturbative statement in the crossover.

This confirms the realistic goal stated in `ROADMAP.md` §6: make the local pieces theorems and isolate D8. It also
names the base case of D8 concretely.
