\subsection{An integrated response criterion for covariance row sums}
\label{ssec:integrated-response}

The slow Gaussian modes in the preceding calculation motivate
a response estimate that keeps track of both time and distance.
Here is an exact sufficient condition, applicable to a finite
Wilson fibre whenever its heat-bath dynamics is ergodic.
It requires no lower bound on the spectral support of a force.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:integrated-response}
Let \(P_i\) be the single-coordinate conditional expectations
on a probability space, with finitely many coordinates, and
let \(\mathscr L=\sum_i(I-P_i)\).
Assume its kernel consists of constants, and write
\(\mathscr P_t=e^{-t\mathscr L}\).
Let \(F_a\) be a finite family of centred real \(L^2\)
observables. Give the indices \(a\) and the coordinates \(i\)
locations in one metric space, with distance \(d\).
For \(\eta\ge0\) define
\[
 \begin{split}
 A_\eta&=\sup_a\sum_i e^{\eta d(a,i)}
                 \|(I-P_i)F_a\|_2,\\
 B_\eta(t)&=\sup_i\sum_b e^{\eta d(i,b)}
                 \|(I-P_i)\mathscr P_tF_b\|_2.
 \end{split}
\]
Then
\[
 \sup_a\sum_b e^{\eta d(a,b)}
           |\operatorname{Cov}(F_a,F_b)|
 \le A_\eta\int_0^\infty B_\eta(t)\,dt .
\]
In particular, if
\(B_\eta(t)\le B_0(1+t/\tau)^{-1-\delta}\)
for \(\tau,\delta>0\), the right side is at most
\(A_\eta B_0\tau/\delta\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Each \(P_i\) is an orthogonal projection in \(L^2\), so
\(\mathscr L\) is bounded, nonnegative and self-adjoint.
Its semigroup converges strongly to the projection onto
the constants by the spectral theorem. Differentiation and
integration of the covariance give
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(F_a,F_b)
 =\int_0^\infty
       \sum_i\langle (I-P_i)F_a,
                    (I-P_i)\mathscr P_tF_b\rangle\,dt .
\]
The identity follows first up to a finite time \(T\);
the remainder is
\(\langle F_a,\mathscr P_TF_b\rangle\to0\).
Absolute integrability follows whenever the displayed bound
is finite. Otherwise the asserted inequality has an infinite
right side and is immediate. Apply Cauchy--Schwarz to each
inner product, use
\(d(a,b)\le d(a,i)+d(i,b)\), sum over \(b,i\), and integrate.
Finally \(\int_0^\infty(1+t/\tau)^{-1-\delta}dt=\tau/\delta\).
\end{proof}

For a sequence of finite Wilson fibres, this would give
vanishing weighted covariance row sums if its response
estimates were uniform in volume and exterior fields and
\(A_\eta B_0\tau\to0\), with a fixed positive \(\delta\).
The proposition proves the implication. The required
Wilson response bound along the continuum tuning is
unproved. It is a stronger requirement than a small
equal-time covariance in a quadratic approximation.
