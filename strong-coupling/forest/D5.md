# D5: from fibre decay to the cube coefficient (November 2026)

This is item 1 of `ROADMAP.md` §7. It has two results and one conclusion.

1. **Theorem D5-calm (§2).** On a calm exterior and a calm fibre, the constrained cube law responds to a change of one
   exterior link with a W₁ coefficient C_G ≤ 3·10¹³, independent of λ, n and the background. There is an additive
   error λ|V|ε_W from the off-chart mass. The proof is complete for exterior links that enter no block average (case
   (a)); for links that enter one, it is only sketched (case (b)). The proof is given here, not in `main.tex`.
2. **Proposition (§3): global calmness is volume-limited.** With today's off-chart bound, the error term is small only
   when β_W ≳ 10³⁹|W|. The class of calm exteriors has Wilson probability ≤ 7·10⁻³²β_W^{3/2}|W|⁻³. Hence D5-calm gives
   D_c(n, α) only as a finite-volume statement at β_W ≈ 10⁹⁷. It gives nothing along the tuning.
3. **Conclusion (§4).** D5 cannot be done globally on the cube. It must be local: the chart and its decay are already
   local, but the off-chart estimate is not. The missing estimate (L) is stated in §4, and it is the same as item 3
   of the roadmap. This merges D5 with D7 and reorders §7.

The labels below are those of `main.tex`.

## 1. What is to be bounded

The objects are those of Theorem `constrainedcube`:
- n is even, V is the set of active links with base in [0, n)⁴, and W is its set of blocks, with |W| = (n/2)⁴ = N_n;
- the averages V_{y,μ}, y ∈ W, are held at v;
- the exterior η is fixed.

The constrained law μ_{η,v} lives on the fibre F(η, v) ⊂ G^{A_W}. The coefficient C^c_{V,j} is the supremum, over
(η, v) and over η′ differing from η only at the exterior link j, of

  W₁^{d_V}(μ_{η,v}, μ_{η′,v}) / d(η_j, η′_j),  with d_V(U, U′) = Σ_{e∈V} d(U_e, U′_e).

D_c(n, α) asks for K^c_c ≤ α N_n. As in part (iii) of the proof of `constrainedcube`, a uniform bound C^c_{V,j} ≤ C gives
K^c_c ≤ (17/n) C N_n for n ≥ 10⁴. Hence D_c(n, α) follows for n ≥ 17C/α.

An exterior link j can enter the cube law in two ways:
- **(a)** through the plaquettes it shares with V;
- **(b)** also through an average: by Lemma `blocktree`(iii), V_{y,μ} contains the links of S^μ_{y+e_μ}, and these
  are exterior when y + e_μ ∉ W.

In case (a) the fibre does not move. In case (b) it does.

## 2. Theorem D5-calm

**Setting.** G = SU(2) and λ = β_W/4. K, s and λ satisfy the hypotheses of Corollary `calmfibres` (those of Theorem
`chartdecay` with K in place of κ, together with s ≥ 1.24·10⁵K). Let η(t), t ∈ [0, 1], be the geodesic from η_j to η′_j
in the link j, with all other exterior links fixed. Hypothesis (H):
- for every t there is a field U^{(t)} ∈ F(η(t), v) whose plaquettes with a vertex in a block of W⁺ are at most
  K/(4√n_P).

If both endpoints have such fields at K/(8√n_P), and d(η_j, η′_j) ≤ K/(8√n_P), then (H) holds along the geodesic.
This uses the same field on A_W, the triangle inequality for δ on the six plaquettes through j, and the bound δ ≤ d.

**Claim (case (a)).** Under (H),

  W₁^{d_V}(μ_{η,v}, μ_{η′,v}) ≤ (C_G + 320 λ|V| ε_W(K, λ)) · d(η_j, η′_j),  with C_G ≤ 3·10¹³.

**Proof.**

In case (a) the fibre F = F(η(t), v) does not depend on t. The law μ_t has density e^{−λQ_t}/Z_t with respect to the
fibre measure ν_c. Only the six plaquettes through j depend on t.

*Step 1: the derivative of the expectation.* Let F be 1-Lipschitz for d_V; it may be taken smooth, because both sides
pass to the limit under uniform approximation. Then

  d/dt E_t[F] = −Cov_{μ_t}(F, G_t),  G_t = λ ∂_t Q_t = λ Σ_{p∋j, p∩V≠∅} ∂_t δ(U_p(t), I)².

This is the covariance under the **full** constrained law. The chart enters only in Step 2, through a decomposition
of this covariance. So no boundary terms of the chart appear.

*Step 2: split off the chart.* Write μ_t = (1−ε)μ^c + εμ^o, where:
- μ^c is μ_t conditioned on the chart Φ_{U^{(t)}}(Ω_r);
- μ^o is μ_t conditioned on the complement of the chart;
- ε ≤ ε_W(K, λ) by Corollary `calmfibres` at U^{(t)}.

Then

  Cov_μ = (1−ε)Cov_{μ^c} + εCov_{μ^o} + ε(1−ε)ΔF·ΔG.

Use the following bounds:
- osc F ≤ |V| diam G ≤ 2π|V|;
- osc G_t ≤ 6λ · osc ∂_t δ² ≤ 34λ, on unit-speed geodesics, since |∂_t δ²| ≤ 2√8 when δ² ≤ 8.

Every covariance is at most osc F osc G/4 in absolute value. Hence

  |Cov_μ(F, G_t) − Cov_{μ^c}(F, G_t)| ≤ ε(¼ + ¼ + 1) osc F osc G ≤ 1.5·2π·34 λ|V| ε_W ≤ 320 λ|V| ε_W.

*Step 3: the chart part, from Theorem `chartdecay`(ii).* Take φ = d(·, j) and α = α_G = 2.5·10⁻⁴. The proof of
`chartdecay`(iii) gives ∇′H = P̄* g, where:
- g_a is the left-invariant derivative of H̃ in link a, multiplied by Ψ(ad_{X_a}), which has norm ≤ 2;
- P̄ satisfies the conjugated bound ‖(P̄*)^{w^{±1}}‖ ≤ p̄_α ≤ 4.8 at α = 2.5·10⁻⁴.

Hence:
- for F: |g_a| ≤ 2 for every a ∈ V, so ‖e^{−αφ}∇′F‖ ≤ 2 p̄_α (Σ_{a∈V} e^{−2αd(a,j)})^{1/2} ≤ 2 p̄_α N(2α_G)^{1/2};
- for G_t: g is supported on the ≤ 18 links of V that share a plaquette with j, and two links share at most one
  plaquette. On that support φ ≤ 1 and |g_a| ≤ 2c_Q λ, where c_Q bounds the mixed second derivative of δ(U_p, I)² in
  two distinct links. With the Frobenius norm on su(2), −2Re Tr(A Y B X C) gives c_Q = 2; we allow c_Q ≤ 4 for the
  norm convention. So ‖e^{αφ}∇′G_t‖ ≤ p̄_α e^{α} √18 · 2c_Q λ.

Insert these in `chartdecay`(ii):

  |Cov_{μ^c}(F, G_t)| ≤ (120/λ) · 2p̄_α N(2α_G)^{1/2} · p̄_α e^{α_G} √18 · 2c_Q λ = C_G.

λ cancels. The count N(2α_G) ≤ 2.46·10¹⁶ uses the shell count of the proof of `constrainedcube`
(`d5/constants.py`, output `d5/constants.out`). It gives C_G ≤ 2.95·10¹³ at c_Q = 4.

*Step 4.* Integrate over t ∈ [0, 1], and take the supremum over F. ∎

**Case (b), sketch only.** When j enters the average V_k of the pair k, change variables with Ψ_t : F(η(0), v) →
F(η(t), v). The map Ψ_t moves only the private path link d_k of k: it solves V_k = v_k for d_k by the contraction
used in the proof of `calmfibre`, which is a contraction with constant (7/8)Λ.

The pulled-back density then picks up:
- the plaquettes through d_k, which are within distance 2 of j;
- the Jacobian of Ψ_t;
- the change of ν_c through |det ∂_g C|.

All three are local near j and of size O(λ) + O(1) per unit t. The term E[∂_t(F∘Ψ_t)] is bounded by the speed of
d_k, which is ≤ 8.01·(1/16)/(1 − (7/8)Λ) per unit speed of η_j. So the same estimate holds with a larger constant.
That constant is **not computed**, and Steps 1–3 have **not been checked** in this case.

**What D5-calm gives.** It gives D_c(n, α) restricted to pairs (η, v) that satisfy (H), for n ≥ 17(C_G + error)/α. In
case (a) that means n ≥ 5·10¹⁴/α. The Gaussian threshold of Theorem `constrainedcube` is 2·10¹⁵/α, so the two are of
the same order. The nonlinear coefficient is as large as the Gaussian one, as expected; the weighted count N(2α_G)
dominates both.

## 3. Proposition: the global version is volume-limited

The error term of §2 is 320λ|V|ε_W, with

  ε_W(K, λ) = (3·10⁸ n_P^{3/2}/K³)^{45|W|} e^{−3λK²/4},  n_P ≤ 384|W|,

where the hypotheses of `calmfibres` force K ≤ 4.9·10⁻¹⁸. Two consequences follow, both immediate from
`main.tex`:

1. **The error is small only if λK² ≳ 60|W| log(n_P/K³).** That is β_W ≳ 1.6·10³⁹|W|, as the paper notes after Corollary `calmfibres`. With n ≈ 5·10¹⁴/α this means
   β_W ≳ 2·10⁹⁷ at α = 0.9 (`d5/constants.out`). So, as a theorem, D5-calm gives D_c(n, α) only on calm pairs, at
   one finite n, with β_W ≈ 10⁹⁷.
2. **The hypothesis class is itself rare.** By Proposition `calmrare`(ii), a calm completion exists with probability
   ≤ 7·10⁻³²β_W^{3/2}|W|⁻³. At the numbers of item 1 this is about 10⁻⁵⁹. Along the tuning, β_W ∼ b log(1/a) while
   |W| grows like a⁻⁴. Both conditions then fail without any margin.

The cause is not a weak constant. The threshold K/(4√n_P) for "calm" shrinks with the volume, and the off-chart bound
compares the whole fibre with a small ball around one good field. Both are global. In the right picture, the
probability that some plaquette of the cube exceeds a fixed K is ≈ |W| e^{−cλK²}. Along the tuning this tends to 1
(Proposition `manydefects`). Any argument that needs the whole cube on one chart therefore fails at the scaling,
however good its constants are.

## 4. Conclusion: D5 has to be local, and then it is D7

What is local and what is not:
- **Local already:**
  - the chart of Theorem `fibrechart`, whose hypothesis (Lemma `smallframes`) is a pointwise bound on plaquettes;
  - the decay of Theorem `chartdecay`, whose constants do not depend on W;
  - the coefficient C_G of §2, which uses only links within the decay length 1/α_G ≈ 4000 of j.
- **Global:**
  - Proposition `calmfibre`, the off-chart mass;
  - the √n_P in the calmness threshold.

So the missing estimate is a localized version of `calmfibre`:

> **(L) Local off-chart bound.** Let B be a ball of radius R ≥ C/α_G around j, with R independent of n. Fix any
> exterior and any v, and condition the constrained law on the links of A_W outside B. If the conditioned fibre
> contains a field whose plaquettes in B are at most K, with K independent of |W|, then the conditional law gives the
> event "some plaquette in B exceeds 2K" mass ≤ C(R) e^{−cλK²}.

Granting (L), the argument of §2, run with the conditional law on B, bounds the response of the links near j. The
response of far links is a sum over paths of local responses, and each path pays e^{−α_G} per step on calm sites and
(4.5β_W)⁴ per site on islands (`D_STATUS.md` §3). That is exactly the Bassalygo–Dobrushin structure of
`D_STATUS.md` §3:
- (G) is §2 together with (L);
- (B) is the island bound transported to chart coordinates (item 4 of the roadmap);
- (P) is the rarity of the islands under an arbitrary exterior. Its constrained-law form is (L) itself (item 3).

The annealed comparison (item 2) is what sums the paths when the islands depend on the field.

**So D5 and D7 are one problem.** It splits into three parts:
- (L), new;
- the annealed comparison;
- the island transport.

§2 is the calm part, and it is done for case (a).

### Effect on the order of work (`ROADMAP.md` §7)

The order stands, with two changes:
- item 3 is now stated precisely, as (L) for the constrained law;
- item 1 is closed as far as it can be closed globally.

(L) is the harder of the remaining items. The paper's energy–entropy argument (Proposition `anyexterior`) is local up
to the boundary layer, and that layer is the obstruction recorded in `RARE.md` §4.
