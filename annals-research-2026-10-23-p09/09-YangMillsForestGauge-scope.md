# Annals class research edition

Prepared in the official `aomart` manuscript class, with full source and figures retained. This is a formatting and bounded-results revision, not a journal acceptance or a certification of every source proof.

The results concern the stated fixed-spacing lattice strong-coupling regime. The continuum Yang-Mills mass gap has not been constructed.

Compile `main-aomart.tex` in its containing directory with pdfLaTeX at least twice. The original package remains unchanged.
